VIX regime — fear is a buy signal, calm is a sell signal

Since 1990 every major VIX spike above 35 marked a multi-month equity buying opportunity, and extended calm below 15 preceded drawdowns. The bands mirror the classic cheat-sheet zones.

Seasonality — when the calendar leans your way

Computed live from the full history loaded above: volatility tends to trough in summer and peak into September–October; Bitcoin's strongest months have historically clustered in Q4 and February, with September the weakest.

Bitcoin 4-year cycle — the slow clock

Halvings (Nov 2012, Jul 2016, May 2020, Apr 2024) have anchored a repeating rhythm: a bull window peaking ~370–550 days after the halving, a bear window bottoming ~780–930 days after, then accumulation and a pre-halving run-up. The overlay indexes each cycle to 100 at its halving so the rhythm is comparable across eras.
